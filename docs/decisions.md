# Design decisions

## A radiating panel

Exciters drive the thin plywood face. This gives the speaker its flat form and makes the material itself part of the sound-producing system. The frame supports the object and its mounting details.

## A distributed mix

The installation separates instrument stems spatially. People move through the room and between the musical parts. The MainStage/receiver chain is recorded separately from the physical panel construction.

## Visible material

The achieved prototype uses hand-finished wood rather than a conventional enclosure and grille. Original build photographs preserve the birch, frame, exciters and finish tests.

## Club and home

The prototype was used at the club event and later in a bedroom. The concept pack extends that thinking to a midcentury living room and kitchen.

## Shared supports

The source design has extended corner tabs. The current concept refines them into precise suspension/resting points, with a centre-back stand and isolated pads. The stand and refined finish are concept work; the original functioning prototype has its own evidence.
