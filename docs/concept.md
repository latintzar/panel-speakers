# Product concept

Panel Speakers uses thin wooden radiating faces to spread musical stems through a club or home. The concept pack is regenerated from the original CAD and photographs of the achieved build.

## Original geometry

The [editable Rhino source](cad.md) retains the saved design revision and its original studies. The prototype recording remains the placement reference for the achieved build.

Two integral drilled wooden tabs extend from one short end of the shallow beech frame. Black loops through those holes carry the suspension. That tabbed end points up when hanging; invert the whole speaker and the same two tabs become its feet. The opposite short end is flush. The radiating face is French-polished birch plywood.

## Exciters

The installed rear view is the placement reference: upper-left and lower-right, offset on the radiating face. The photographed parts have amber fabric spiders, coil formers, terminal plates and black magnet backs. The black magnet backs face outward when installed; the wiring and driver positions rotate with the panel when it is inverted. Exact measured coordinates belong in the next CAD revision.

## Contexts

The club and bedroom installations were physically achieved and are shown in the original [evidence](evidence.md). The home-listening proposal adds a centre-back support with acoustic isolation, allowing an inverted panel to lean on its original tabs.
