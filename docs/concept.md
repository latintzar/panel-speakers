# Product concept

A thin wooden panel can live around a DJ set or in a home. The sound-radiating surface remains visually quiet; the structure and suspension stay legible.

The concept pack develops a restrained satin walnut finish, slim open rear supports, small corner suspension eyes and matching resting feet. A centre-back stand hinges from the frame and lets a panel lean on isolated contact pads, leaving the radiating face clear.

These finish and stand refinements are concept work. The achieved build used French-polished birch faces and beech frames; its actual photographs and recordings are the prototype evidence.
