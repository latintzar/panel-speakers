# Prototype build

The original system was built in two weeks for the Architectural Association’s club night at Egg London. Four radiating panels were cut, assembled, fitted with exciters and hand French-polished.

## Panel and frame

The case records 4 mm birch plywood faces bonded to beech timber frames from Hooke Park. The shallow open frame carries the mounting points and terminals. The thin face is the sound-radiating element.

[Cut panels and parts](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps_b_ply.jpg) · [Unfinished assembly](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps_b_prepolish.jpg)

## Exciters and connection

Two Dayton Audio EX32VBDS-4 exciters are recorded for each panel. The original rear footage shows them diagonally offset and connected by red/black speaker leads. The manufacturer specifies a 32 mm voice coil, 40 W RMS handling and 4 Ω impedance for each exciter; system loading depends on its wiring.

[Manufacturer specification](https://www.daytonaudio.com/product/1961/ex32vbds-4-ims-dual-suspension-vented-bass-32mm-interchangeable-hardware-mount-exciter-40w-4-ohm) · [Original rear build recording](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps06.mp4)

## Finish

The achieved panels were hand French-polished. The original record describes roughly four hours of polishing across all four panels. The source photographs preserve the amber birch finish and the material tests.

[Finish test](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps_b_polish_test.jpg) · [Polishing](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps_b_polish_selfie.jpg) · [Finished panels](https://atelierneyra.com/hardware/media/ps10.jpg)

## Listening and installation

The panels played individual stems across a multichannel system at the event. Later recordings show a pair used in a bedroom. A foam-layer experiment is retained as a qualitative listening/build observation.

The [original Rhino model](cad.md) records the saved panel geometry. The next technical records can add as-built measurements, a wiring drawing, controlled response measurements and mounting/stand tests. The original working build and the later concept refinement remain separately documented.
